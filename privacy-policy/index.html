Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 17, 2026 · Last Updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy outlines how Nihar Ranjan Rout (Founder & CEO of Creuto, operating via niharrout.com) collects, handles, uses, and safeguards personal information and project data provided by visitors, founders, and prospective clients.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect personal information that you voluntarily provide when engaging with the site’s interactive features, lead generation tools, or direct communications:

  • Contact Information: Your name, business work email address, telephone/mobile number, and business or company name.
  • Project Scoping Details: Product concepts, industry sector, team size, technical requirements, project scale, target timeline, and budget ranges submitted through our Strategy Consultation form, Founder Suite form, or "What Would This Cost?" scope estimator.
  • Communication Records: Details included in email inquiries or direct scheduling requests.
  • Automated Log & Technical Data: Standard server access logs including IP address, browser type, referral URLs, and pages visited to ensure network security and optimal performance.

2. How We Use Your Information

Information submitted through this website is strictly used for legitimate business purposes:

  • To personally review your software requirements, draft preliminary PRD scoping notes, and prepare realistic project roadmaps and budget estimates.
  • To respond to your inquiries and schedule strategy consultations within 24 hours.
  • To execute software design, architecture, and development services through Creuto where a contractual engagement is agreed upon.
  • To maintain platform security, prevent automated spam submissions, and uphold data integrity.
Zero Sale of Personal Data: We do not sell, trade, rent, or monetize your contact information or project ideas to third parties, data brokers, or advertisers under any circumstances.

3. Confidentiality & Non-Disclosure (NDAs)

We treat early-stage product concepts, architecture diagrams, and business specifications with the strictest professional confidentiality. If your team requires an executed bilateral Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) prior to detailed scoping or code discussions, standard mutual NDAs are provided and signed upon request.

4. Third-Party Services & Infrastructure

To deliver a fast, reliable, and secure web experience, niharrout.com integrates select vetted service providers:

  • Hosting & DNS: Secure CDN and web servers running automated HTTPS encryption.
  • Typography: Google Fonts CDN (Manrope, JetBrains Mono) loaded via secure endpoints.
  • Verified Client Reviews: Clutch.co embedded review widget for displaying verified third-party client testimonials.
  • Weather & Local Time Widget: The homepage contact section shows the current weather in Bhubaneswar, fetched from Open-Meteo using fixed coordinates. The request contains nothing about you. The local-time comparison is calculated in your browser from your device's time zone setting and is not sent anywhere.
  • Analytics (Google Analytics 4): We use Google Analytics to understand how the site is used: pages viewed, how visitors arrive, and which contact buttons are tapped. It uses cookies and shares anonymised usage data with Google. We do not use it to identify you personally. If you are in Europe, the UK or a nearby time zone, Google Analytics is not loaded at all until you press Accept on the cookie banner. You can change your choice at any time through “Cookie settings” in the footer, or block it with a browser setting or Google’s Analytics opt-out add-on.
  • Video (YouTube): Videos are embedded in click-to-load mode using YouTube's privacy-enhanced domain. Nothing is requested from YouTube until you press play.
  • Scheduling (Calendly): The “Book a call” button opens Calendly, which handles your booking details under its own privacy policy.
  • Notification Webhooks: Encrypted operational notification channels to ensure fast 24-hour response times to founder inquiries.

5. Data Retention & Security

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the inquiries or project engagements for which it was collected, or to comply with statutory legal and accounting obligations. We implement rigorous administrative, physical, and technical safeguards to prevent unauthorized access, disclosure, or alteration of your personal data.

6. Your Rights Regarding Your Data

Under applicable data protection regulations (including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act and international standards such as GDPR where applicable), you possess the right to:

  • Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction or rectification of incomplete or inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion or erasure of your contact records and scoping submissions from our active systems.
  • Withdraw consent to communications at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, please email us directly at me@niharrout.com.

Any disputes arising out of the use of this website or this Privacy Policy shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of India, subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the competent courts in Bhubaneswar, Odisha.
