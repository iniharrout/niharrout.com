Skribe — Team Chat That Meets You Where You Already Are

One chat layer for teams split across Slack-style channels, Google Chat, Google Workspace, and Microsoft 365.

The Problem Space

The Challenge

Most companies aren't actually on one communication tool anymore — one team lives on Google Chat, another runs on Microsoft 365, and getting a message across the org means either forcing everyone onto the same platform or watching messages fall through the cracks between tools no one wants to give up.

Strategic Scoping

The Product Decision

Rather than build another standalone team-chat app asking companies to migrate away from tools they'd already standardized on, Skribe was scoped as a connective layer: native team chat that also bridges into Google Chat, Google Workspace, and Microsoft 365 — so a message reaches people wherever they already are, instead of creating one more inbox for everyone to check. The product promise: "Reach with clarity. Know what worked."

System Architecture

What We Built

A breakdown of the modular components engineered and shipped into production.

01

High-Performance Native Team Chat

Topic-based channels, direct 1-to-1 messages, rich markdown formatting, searchable message history, and instant desktop/web notifications.

02

Bi-Directional Google Chat Sync

Messages sent in Skribe sync seamlessly into Google Chat spaces and direct messages without latency or dropped attachments.

03

Deep Google Workspace Directory Integration

Connects directly with corporate Google Workspace accounts, organization organizational units (OUs), and automated single sign-on (SSO).

04

Microsoft 365 & Teams Interoperability

Reaches business units already standardized on Microsoft 365 without forcing an organizational tool migration.

05

Message Delivery & Action Clarity

Transparent read status, acknowledgment receipts, and engagement analytics so leadership knows communications landed.

06

Enterprise Security & Compliance Controls

End-to-end data encryption in transit and at rest, retention policies, and administrative audit logging for corporate compliance.

Measurable Outcomes

Verified Business Impact

✓ Unifies native chat, Google Chat, Google Workspace, and Microsoft 365 in a single thread
✓ Eliminates communication drop-offs between distributed enterprise teams
✓ Zero employee friction by meeting users in their preferred workplace software
✓ Sub-second message dispatch and synchronized push notifications across web and desktop
