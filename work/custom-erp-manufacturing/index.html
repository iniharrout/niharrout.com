The Problem Space

The Challenge

HR, sales, inventory, and finance were each running on their own fragmented tools with no shared data layer — every cross-team task meant manual reconciliation. Attendance was tracked by hand with no payroll link. Sales kept separate lead files. Inventory ran on outdated spreadsheets. Finance compiled reports manually. There were no role-based permissions, so sensitive data was visible to everyone — and leadership had no real-time view of the business.

Strategic Scoping

The Product Decision

Rather than patch each department's tool individually, we scoped a single PRD to unify HR, CRM/Sales, Inventory, and Finance on one platform with role-based dashboards from day one — then built it module by module in two-week sprints, with weekly touchpoints per department so nothing shipped that the actual users hadn't already validated.

System Architecture

What We Built

A breakdown of the modular components engineered and shipped into production.

01

HR Management

Real-time attendance, leave, and movement tracking with location-based and QR check-in, streamlined employee onboarding/offboarding, and automated digital performance reviews linked directly to payroll.

02

CRM & Sales Pipeline

Unified customer follow-up and field visit logging, automated quotation and commercial approval management, and team-specific pipeline access controls with conversion analytics.

03

Inventory & Supply Chain

Real-time warehouse stock and daily sales visibility, multi-vendor purchase order (PO) and bill management, automated low-stock threshold alerts, and reorder triggers.

04

Finance & Expenses

Centralized income/expense recording, automated P&L reconciliation, department-level cost center audits, and monthly financial close reduced from a multi-day ordeal to a one-day review.

05

Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)

Personalized dashboards per operational role (floor supervisor, sales rep, plant manager, CFO) with granular permissions protecting sensitive commercial and salary data.

06

Unified Leadership Reporting

One real-time cross-functional executive dashboard providing instant visibility into manufacturing throughput, cash flow, order fulfillment latency, and labor efficiency.
