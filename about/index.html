Chapter 01

Eight years at the intersection of business, product, and code.

Before founding Creuto, I spent eight years immersed in product management across diverse industries: B2B SaaS, enterprise ERPs, consumer marketplaces, logistics engines, and AI applications.

Across dozens of software builds, a single pattern became undeniable: most software fails for a product reason, not a technical one.

Teams don't fail because their developers couldn't configure a database or write an API endpoint. They fail because nobody stopped to ask whether the feature should exist in the first place. They build the wrong scope, misjudge user workflows, over-engineer premature features, and exhaust their investment before validating real demand.

“Code is the most expensive way to test an assumption. Product judgment ensures you only build what actually matters.”

Nihar Ranjan Rout
Chapter 02

Why Creuto was built: fixing the broken agency model.

The traditional software development agency model is fundamentally misaligned with founders. In most agencies, senior partners pitch impressive presentations to win the contract, and the moment the invoice is signed, the project is quietly handed down to junior developers or third-party freelancers with zero context.

The agency gets paid for hours billed, meaning they are financially incentivized to let scope balloon and prolong delivery.

I started Creuto as the antidote to that model: an in-house software engineering company led directly by product management.

01

An In-House Team

The engineers, designers and QA specialists on your build are part of Creuto’s in-house team, led directly by Nihar. One team, one person accountable.

02

Product Discipline First

We craft a comprehensive PRD (Product Requirement Document) before writing code, locking scope and prioritizing high-impact features.

03

Milestone Pricing

Predictable fixed-milestone pricing tied to approved deliverables. You never receive surprise invoices or runaway hourly bills.

Chapter 03

The Product-First Philosophy.

At Creuto, we treat your capital with the same discipline we’d treat our own. When you come to us with a concept, we don't blindly nod and generate a bloated quote. We interrogate the problem space:

  • What is the single core user action that proves this software is valuable?
  • What manual workflow or departmental friction are we actually solving?
  • Which 80% of suggested features can be postponed to V2 without hurting launch adoption?
  • What pragmatic tech stack delivers the highest reliability at the lowest maintenance cost?

This product-first rigor protects your runway, eliminates scope bloat, and delivers software that feels intuitive and refined from day one.

Chapter 04

What working together looks like day-to-day.

When you partner with us, you aren’t routed through account managers or junior liaisons. You work directly with me on product strategy, architecture, and roadmap scoping, backed by Creuto’s engineering engine.

1

PRD & Architecture Blueprint

We map functional specifications, database models, user flows, and milestone budgets so everyone is aligned before coding starts.

2

Regular Demo Sprints

You see real working software on live staging servers at every milestone. No slide decks, no excuses, just measurable progress.

3

Time-Zone Alignment

Asynchronous daily updates via Loom and GitHub, plus agreed live overlap hours each week for standups and demos with US, EU, and APAC founders.

4

Code handover

Your repositories, design files and database schemas sit in your own accounts from day one, and ownership of the custom work passes to you as each milestone is paid for.
