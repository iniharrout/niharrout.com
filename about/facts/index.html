Frequently asked questions

Who runs Creuto?

Creuto is led by founder and CEO Nihar Ranjan Rout, who has 8+ years in product management and works directly with every client.

Where is Creuto based?

Creuto is based in Bhubaneswar, Odisha, India, and works with clients around the world, including the US, UK, Australia, Vietnam and India.

What does Creuto build?

Mobile apps, web applications, custom software and ERP systems, AI products, and product strategy documents (PRDs) with clickable prototypes.

Who owns the code Creuto writes?

The client. The source code, database schemas, deployment pipelines and design assets belong to the client from day one and are kept in the client’s own accounts.

How does Creuto price projects?

With fixed milestones tied to agreed deliverables, after a PRD and prototype define the scope. There is no surprise hourly billing.

What happens after launch?

Every production launch includes a 60-day warranty covering bug fixes, monitoring and performance tuning, with optional maintenance after that.

Want to talk to the founder?

The first call is free and takes 45 minutes. You get a clear proposal within 24 hours.
