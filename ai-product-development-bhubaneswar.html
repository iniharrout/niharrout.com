How much does it cost to build a custom AI product or MVP in Bhubaneswar?
A production-ready AI MVP (such as an Enterprise RAG chatbot, custom document processing engine, or AI agent workflow) typically ranges between ₹2.5 Lakhs to ₹7 Lakhs ($3,000 - $8,500 USD). Large-scale enterprise AI software with custom fine-tuning, multi-agent pipelines, and multi-tenant billing ranges between ₹8 Lakhs to ₹20+ Lakhs. We offer fixed milestone pricing after an initial architecture review.
How do you prevent hallucinations in generative AI systems?
We implement multi-stage verification: strictly grounded Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) with citation mapping, low-temperature prompt constraints, Guardrails (NeMo & LlamaGuard), and self-reflection loops where the model audits its own response against the retrieved source passages before displaying it to the user.
Is my proprietary company data safe from training public models?
Yes. We strictly use enterprise API endpoints (OpenAI Enterprise, AWS Bedrock, Anthropic Claude Commercial) which legally guarantee that your data is never used to train foundational models. For highly sensitive requirements (banking, defense, healthcare), we deploy private, self-hosted open-weight models (Llama 3, Mistral) inside your own dedicated cloud VPC.
How fast can we launch an AI MVP?
Most AI MVPs are scoped, designed, built, benchmarked, and deployed in 4 to 8 weeks. We leverage modular backend accelerators (FastAPI, LangGraph, Vector DB adapters) so you don't spend months reinventing the wheel.
