Creuto Is Now an OpenAI Select Partner

Building AI That Actually Works: Why the OpenAI Select Partner Status Matters More to Me Than It Looks

Nihar Ranjan Rout
Founder & CEO, Creuto
Published September 21, 2026 · 3 min read
Announcement
Creuto is now an OpenAI Select Partner
Company
Creuto, led by founder Nihar Ranjan Rout
Announced
September 21, 2026
What it means
Direct access to OpenAI's frontier models and closer alignment with responsible enterprise deployment

There's a version of this post that writes itself — "thrilled to announce," a few adjectives, a badge graphic, done. That's not the post I want to write.

Creuto is now an OpenAI Select Partner. I want to explain why that matters to me, not just what it is.

The question that changed

For a long time, the first question in almost every client conversation was some version of "does AI actually work for this?" People were curious, skeptical, a little hopeful, mostly unsure. That question has quietly disappeared. What's replaced it is harder: "how do we put this into production without it breaking, without it being a security risk, without it falling apart the moment real users touch it?"

That's a different problem. It's not a technology problem anymore — everyone has access to the same models. It's an engineering and discipline problem. And it's the problem I've spent the last several years building Creuto around.

What "Select Partner" actually means to me

I could describe the OpenAI Partner Network in program terms — it's a group OpenAI works with directly to help organizations adopt its models responsibly, at scale. All true, and you can read that on their site.

What it means to me is simpler: someone outside our own walls looked closely at how we build and decided it holds up. That's a different kind of validation than a client testimonial or a case study we wrote ourselves. It's not something I can talk our way into. It's earned through the actual work — the projects that shipped, the systems that are still running in production today, the mistakes we didn't repeat twice.

Why I'm not going to oversell this

I've seen enough "we're excited to announce our AI partnership" posts to know how quickly that language becomes noise. This isn't a pivot. We didn't just discover AI. It's a marker on a path we were already on — one more proof point that the standard we've held ourselves to is the right one.

If anything, this raises the bar we hold ourselves to, not the other way around.

What changes from here

Practically, it means our team builds with direct access to OpenAI's frontier models and closer alignment with how OpenAI expects enterprises to deploy them responsibly. For the organizations we work with, it means the trust question — can we actually rely on this team — has an answer that doesn't require just taking our word for it.

For me personally, it's a good moment to say clearly what I believe: the companies that win the next few years of AI won't be the ones with the best pitch. They'll be the ones who can quietly, reliably, make it work.

That's the company I'm trying to build.
