FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Common questions about web application development.

Straightforward answers on cost, timelines, SEO, and ownership.

A website mostly displays content. A web application lets a logged-in user do something: book, pay, manage data, or collaborate in real time, with its own database, authentication, and business logic behind it. Most of what we build sits on that application side, customer portals, admin dashboards, and booking or marketplace platforms, rather than brochure sites.

Cost depends on scope, and our estimator is based on products we have delivered. A focused first version typically runs ₹2.5L to ₹5L ($3k to $6k) over 4 to 7 weeks. A standard product with several user roles, payments and an admin console runs ₹5L to ₹12L ($6k to $15k) over 8 to 14 weeks. A larger platform starts around ₹12L ($15k) and takes 3 to 5 months. See the project costs guide for a range based on your requirements; we confirm a fixed-scope price after a discovery call.

When it fits the use case, yes. A Progressive Web App installs to a home screen, works offline, and sends push notifications, without an App Store or Play Store review cycle. It is a strong fit for internal tools and fast-moving MVPs. For consumer apps that need deep device integration or App Store distribution, we build natively in React Native instead, and we will tell you plainly which path fits your product before you commit to either.

Yes, where it matters. Public-facing pages are server-rendered with Next.js so Google indexes real content on first crawl, not a blank loading screen, and we ship clean metadata, structured data, and Core Web Vitals performance budgets from the first sprint. Logged-in application screens are not indexed, since there is nothing for a search engine to rank there.

You do. You own every repository, design file, and database schema, and everything is transferred to your organization at milestone sign-off. There is no vendor lock-in.
