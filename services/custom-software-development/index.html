FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Common questions about custom software & ERP development.

Straightforward answers regarding total cost of ownership, migration safety, and ongoing support.

Off-the-shelf ERP platforms charge massive annual per-user licensing fees ($150–$300/user/month), require expensive certified consultants for minor customizations, and force your business to conform to rigid software conventions. Custom software requires an upfront capital investment but incurs zero recurring seat licenses, perfectly mirrors your proprietary competitive advantages, and belongs 100% to your balance sheet as a corporate asset.

We utilize the Strangler Fig architectural pattern combined with dual-track ETL replication. Both old and new systems run concurrently during a 2 to 4 week validation window. Transactions are verified on both ledgers before flipping the primary operational switch on a scheduled maintenance weekend.

A phased implementation strategy typically launches the core Phase 1 operational module (e.g. inventory tracking or order dispatch) within 10 to 14 weeks. Subsequent departmental modules roll out incrementally every 4 to 6 weeks, allowing your staff to adapt smoothly without operational chaos.
