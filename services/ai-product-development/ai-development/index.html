How we approach Artificial Intelligence Development

We choose one narrow use case and build a prototype against your real data within the first weeks, because that is where assumptions break. We measure it on a set of real questions, add guardrails and sources, and only then build the surrounding product. Privacy, access control and running cost are agreed in the PRD, before any model is chosen.

A good fit when

Where do we start?

With one narrow use case where success is easy to measure. We prototype it quickly, test it on your real data and only then build it out.

Is our data safe?

We design for it: access control, no training on your data by default, choice of providers and, where required, private deployments. Privacy is agreed before building.

By answering from your documents, showing sources, testing against known questions and adding checks that refuse to answer when the evidence is thin.
