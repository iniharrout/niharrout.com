FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know about our mobile process.

Clear answers on timelines, tech selection, IP ownership, and Apple/Google review criteria.

If you are building a product that requires equal feature parity on both iOS and Android (e.g. marketplaces, social apps, fintech, SaaS portals), React Native or Flutter is the most cost-effective and fastest route, saving 35-40% in engineering budget with 90%+ shared code. If your app requires heavy custom hardware interactions (BLE firmware sync, heavy ARKit/Metal shaders, or continuous background GPS telemetry), 100% native Swift and Kotlin is the superior choice. We help you choose the right path during Phase 1.

A focused, high-quality MVP typically launches in 10 to 12 weeks. Complex enterprise mobile applications featuring offline synchronization, complex role-based permissions, and custom ERP connectors take 14 to 18 weeks. Every project is scoped into 2-week sprints with deliverables you can test on your phone.

You own 100% of the code, designs, tokens, documentation, and IP from day one. All repositories are maintained directly under your organization's GitHub or GitLab account. We never lock clients into proprietary hosting frameworks.
