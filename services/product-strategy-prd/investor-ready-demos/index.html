How we approach Investor-Ready Demos

We work backwards from the story you need to tell in a short meeting and decide which two or three flows prove it. Those flows are built to work reliably with realistic data, and we rehearse the demo path, including what to do if something fails. We tell you exactly what is real and what is simulated, so you can answer diligence questions honestly.

When this is a good fit

A good fit when

  • You are preparing to raise and want more than slides.
  • You need a stable demo for pitches, accelerators or pilot customers.
  • You want investors to see a credible path to the full product.

Questions about Investor-Ready Demos

Is the demo real software or a mock-up?

Often a mix: the key flows work for real, and the rest uses realistic sample data. We tell you exactly what is real so you can answer questions honestly.

How long does it take?

It depends on the story you need to tell. A focused demo is much quicker than a full MVP, and we scope it together.

Can the demo become the product?

Yes, when we build it on proper foundations. That is usually the better route compared with a throwaway demo.
