How We Eliminate Budget Surprises

The biggest hidden cost in software development is building the wrong features. Here is how Creuto ensures your investment converts into measurable product value.

01 / PRD-First Alignment

Structured PRD Before Code

Before writing a single line of code, we align on user personas, explicit edge cases, data flows, and acceptance criteria. A comprehensive PRD prevents the mid-sprint scope creep that inflates bills by 2x–3x.

02 / Milestone-Based Delivery

Fixed Milestone Pricing

We don't bill endless vague hourly invoices. Every phase is broken into two-week sprints with predefined deliverables, functional demos, and testable milestones you approve before moving forward.

03 / In-House Senior Team

100% In-House Engineering

Creuto does not outsource or pass your project off to junior freelancers. You collaborate directly with Nihar and our senior engineering team who own the code quality from architectural diagram to production release.
