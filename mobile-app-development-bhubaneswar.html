How much does it cost to develop a mobile app in Bhubaneswar? +
The cost of mobile app development in Bhubaneswar depends on complexity, platform choice (iOS, Android, or Cross-Platform Flutter/React Native), backend integrations, and custom features. A standard MVP typically ranges between ₹1.5 Lakhs to ₹5 Lakhs ($2,000 - $6,000 USD), while complex enterprise mobile systems range from ₹6 Lakhs to ₹15+ Lakhs. We provide fixed-price quotes after a detailed PRD session so there are zero surprise costs.
How long does it take to build and launch an MVP mobile app? +
A focused Minimum Viable Product (MVP) mobile app usually takes 4 to 8 weeks from PRD sign-off to App Store & Google Play Store submission. Larger enterprise applications with custom backend APIs, payment integrations, and admin dashboards take between 10 to 16 weeks.
Should I choose Native (Swift/Kotlin) or Cross-Platform (Flutter/React Native)? +
For 85% of startups and businesses, Cross-Platform development with Flutter or React Native is the best choice because it cuts costs and development time by almost 40% while maintaining native 60fps performance on both iOS and Android. If your app requires heavy hardware access (e.g. low-level Bluetooth, ARKit/ARCore, or high-end 3D graphics), we recommend native Swift and Kotlin.
