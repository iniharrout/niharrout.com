Growth Playbook

How to Win the LinkedIn Algorithm with Clean Formatting

Formatting on LinkedIn is not merely cosmetic. It is an algorithmic lever designed to capture cognitive attention in high-velocity mobile feeds.

1. The 3-Line Mobile Cutoff Rule

Over 78% of LinkedIn impressions originate on mobile devices where the feed folds post text after just 3 short lines (~140 characters) before displaying the "...see more" button.

If your opening doesn't spark intense curiosity, state a counter-intuitive premise, or outline concrete business impact within those 140 characters, users will scroll past without clicking.

2. Strategic Bold Emphasis

Use Bold Sans or Bold Serif sparingly. Highlighting 1 or 2 pivotal keywords (such as key metrics, pain points, or unexpected verbs) directs the reader's eye pattern smoothly down the page.

Avoid turning whole paragraphs into bold Unicode text, as screen readers may announce individual mathematical alphanumeric symbols sequentially.

3. White Space & Bullet Cadence

Dense walls of text choke read-through rates. Break ideas into single-sentence assertions separated by double line returns.

When presenting steps or takeaways, use clean bullet markers (•) or numbered indicators (1. 2. 3.) to create skimmable visual milestones.
