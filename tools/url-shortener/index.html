    How it works

    Your link is sent to a public shortening service (TinyURL, with is.gd as a backup) and the short link comes back to you. Short links do not expire, and they open the original page with a normal redirect.

    Good to know

    Short links are on the provider’s domain, not this site. Do not use them for spam, phishing or anything illegal. The provider can disable links that break its rules. Please do not shorten private links, because anyone with the short link can open the page.
