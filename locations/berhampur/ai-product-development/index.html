Why Berhampur businesses choose Creuto for AI product development

Berhampur (Brahmapur) is the commercial hub of southern Odisha, roughly 170 km from Bhubaneswar in Ganjam district. Silk and handloom textiles, coastal trade and seafood around the Gopalpur port belt, and a large medical and education cluster drive the local economy.

For Berhampur, AI is most useful where language and images are the bottleneck: reading regional-language documents, grading product quality from photos, and answering student or customer queries around the clock in Odia.
