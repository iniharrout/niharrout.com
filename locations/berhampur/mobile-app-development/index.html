Serving Berhampur (Brahmapur) from Bhubaneswar

Why Berhampur businesses choose Creuto for mobile app development

Berhampur (Brahmapur) is the commercial hub of southern Odisha, roughly 170 km from Bhubaneswar in Ganjam district. Silk and handloom textiles, coastal trade and seafood around the Gopalpur port belt, and a large medical and education cluster drive the local economy.

Berhampur’s weavers, saree traders and seafood exporters sell to buyers far from Ganjam. A well-built app lets them show catalogues, take advance orders and collect payments without depending on a middleman or a marketplace.
