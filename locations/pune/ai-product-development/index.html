Why Pune businesses choose Creuto for AI product development

Pune pairs a deep IT and product-engineering talent pool in Hinjewadi and Kharadi with one of India’s strongest automotive and manufacturing belts across Chakan, Pimpri-Chinchwad and Talegaon. Manufacturers and mid-market firms here increasingly want to digitise shop-floor and supply-chain workflows.

In Pune’s manufacturing belt, AI value shows up in quality inspection, demand planning and maintenance. In its IT base, it shows up in shipping AI features faster. We start with the use case where a measurable saving already exists.
