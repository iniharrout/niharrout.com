Why Cuttack businesses choose Creuto for AI product development

Cuttack is Odisha’s traditional commercial centre, about 30 km from Bhubaneswar. Its economy runs on wholesale trade around Badambadi and Buxi Bazaar, silver filigree and handicraft exports, textiles, and a large base of legal, medical and education institutions.

For Cuttack businesses the first AI win is rarely a chatbot. It is turning piles of invoices, ledgers, case papers or handwritten registers into structured data, and answering repeat customer questions automatically in the language customers use.

A filigree exporter reads buyer enquiries in three languages

A 4-week pilot would classify each enquiry, extract product, quantity and deadline into a sheet, and draft a first reply for the owner to approve.
