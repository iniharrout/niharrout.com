Questions before you apply

Are these jobs remote?

Yes. All four roles are remote and open to people based anywhere in India.

Are the roles full-time?

Yes. All four are full-time roles.

How do I apply?

Open a role, read the details and press Apply. The form asks for your contact details, your LinkedIn profile and a link to your work. Applications are sent to me directly.

What happens after I apply?

Applications are sent to me directly. If one looks like a fit, I will get in touch by email or WhatsApp.

Do I need a portfolio?

For the graphic designer and video editor roles, yes: share a link to your work. For the LinkedIn and content role, links to posts you have written help. For Founder’s Office, a clear note about what you have done is the most useful.
