How we work across sectors

Every sector has its own vocabulary, rules and risks, but the way we start is the same. We spend time with the people who do the work, write down how things actually run, and turn that into a PRD before building anything. Where regulation applies, such as health data, payments or public procurement, we design for the requirements you give us and support your own compliance review. The case studies on this site show what we have built and how.

Questions about industries

Do you only work in the industries listed?

No. The list shows the sectors we are asked about most. If your industry is not here, the way we work still applies: start with the people who do the work, write a PRD and build in stages. Tell us about your project and we will say honestly whether we are a fit.

Do you have experience in my industry?

Our published case studies cover manufacturing, event and venue booking, beauty and wellness booking, quick commerce and team communication software. For other sectors we bring the same process and ask you for the domain knowledge, which we capture in the PRD.

How do you handle industry regulations?

We design for the requirements you share, such as data protection, payment or accessibility rules, and build the access control, logging and records they call for. Your compliance team or legal adviser remains responsible for sign-off.

Not sure where you fit?

Tell me what you want to build and I will tell you honestly whether we are the right team.
