Logistics & Supply Chain software development

Logistics is about knowing where everything is and what happens next. We build the dispatch, tracking and warehouse tools that give operations that view in real time.

Book a free strategy call See our work

What we build for Logistics & Supply Chain

  • 01

    Fleet and delivery tracking

    Live location, routes, proof of delivery and customer notifications.

  • 02

    Dispatch and routing

    Assign jobs, optimise routes and handle changes during the day.

  • 03

    Warehouse management

    Receiving, put-away, picking, packing and stock counts with handheld scanning.

  • 04

    Order and shipment portals

    Customers and partners see status without calling.

  • 05

    Supplier and procurement tools

    Purchase orders, approvals and delivery follow-up.

  • 06

    Operations dashboards

    Delivery performance, delays and capacity in one view.

How we approach Logistics & Supply Chain projects

We start on the ground, with the drivers, dispatchers and warehouse staff, because the real constraints are phones with weak signal and exceptions that happen all day. Offline-capable mobile apps and a clear exception workflow come first, with live tracking and reporting built on the same data. Integrations with your ERP and carriers follow in stages.

What matters in Logistics & Supply Chain

  • 01

    Real-time data

    Operations act on live information, so the system has to be fast and dependable.

  • 02

    Field reality

    Drivers and warehouse staff work on phones and scanners, often with poor signal, so offline support matters.

  • 03

    Integration

    ERP, carriers, marketplaces and telematics devices all have to talk to each other.

  • 04

    Exceptions

    Most work is handling what goes wrong, so the tools are built around exceptions.

Questions about Logistics & Supply Chain software

Can you build driver and field apps?

Yes. We build mobile apps for drivers and warehouse staff, with offline support and barcode scanning where needed.

Can you integrate GPS and telematics devices?

Usually yes, through the device or platform APIs. We confirm the data available during scoping.

Can this connect to our ERP?

Yes. Orders, stock and invoices can flow between the logistics tools and your ERP or accounting system.
