Education & EdTech software development

Education software has to be simple enough for students and parents and flexible enough for institutions. We build learning platforms and school systems that people actually finish using.

Book a free strategy call See our work

What we build for Education & EdTech

  • 01

    Learning platforms

    Courses, lessons, quizzes, progress tracking and certificates.

  • 02

    Live classes and tutoring

    Scheduling, video sessions, whiteboards and recordings.

  • 03

    School and college management

    Admissions, attendance, fee collection, timetables and parent communication.

  • 04

    Assessment and exam tools

    Question banks, timed tests, grading and analytics.

  • 05

    Student and parent apps

    Notifications, results, homework and messaging.

  • 06

    AI tutors and study assistants

    Explanations, practice questions and feedback based on your content.

How we approach Education & EdTech projects

We begin with the learner and the teacher, and design the simplest path for each. Content structure, progress tracking and low-bandwidth performance come first. Where children are involved, we plan consent and data handling at the start. Assessments and live sessions are tested under realistic peak loads, such as exam day, before release.

What matters in Education & EdTech

  • 01

    Ease of use

    Students and teachers of every age and skill level must be able to use it without training.

  • 02

    Low bandwidth

    Many learners use phones on slow connections, so offline and light pages matter.

  • 03

    Privacy of minors

    Children's data needs extra care and clear parental consent flows.

  • 04

    Seasonal peaks

    Exams and admissions create sudden load that the platform has to handle.

Questions about Education & EdTech software

Can you build a platform for live and recorded classes?

Yes. We can integrate video services or build the scheduling, attendance and recording features around them.

Can you handle student data safely?

Yes. We use role-based access, encryption and sensible retention rules, and support requirements for children's data where they apply.

Can you add AI to our content?

Yes. Assistants that answer from your own course material, with sources, are a common and useful addition.
