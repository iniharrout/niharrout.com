Do you help define the product, or only build it?

Both — and the product comes first. With eight years in product management, I help you shape scope, priorities, and a clear PRD before any code is written. Once the product is right, my team at Creuto engineers it. You can also engage us purely for product consulting if that's all you need.

How do you keep a project strictly within budget?

By scoping honestly up front. A good PRD prevents the biggest cost driver in software — building the wrong thing. We plan a build that fits what you can invest, prioritize what matters first, and are transparent about trade-offs. No inflated quotes, no surprise costs.

What is the relationship between Nihar and Creuto?

I am the Founder & CEO of Creuto. Creuto is my company and dedicated in-house engineering team. I personally lead all product strategy, PRD definition, AI feasibility, and architecture. My in-house engineering team at Creuto then designs, builds, and deploys the production-grade software under my direct supervision.

Who owns the source code and IP?

You do, 100%. Upon completion and handover, all source code, Figma design assets, databases, and IP belong entirely to your company with zero vendor lock-in.
