Is this a fit?

Who I work with, and who I don’t.

If this sounds like you, let’s talk. If it does not, I will tell you honestly on the first call.

A good fit if…

  • Founders with a product idea and no technical team.
  • Businesses replacing spreadsheets, manual processes or ageing software.
  • Teams adding AI to a product or workflow they already run.
  • People who want to deal with the person building it, not an account manager.

Probably not a fit if…

  • You want the lowest possible quote. We run a senior in-house team, so we are rarely the cheapest.
  • You want to hire developers by the hour and manage them yourself. We work in fixed milestones with an agreed scope.
  • You need something built with no time for planning. We write the plan before we build.

My principles

The principles I live and work by.

Software has felt like magic to me since I was a small boy. Someone touches a button and things just happen. I could never leave it there. I wanted to know how it worked, and then how to use it to solve the next problem. That curiosity is still what I bring to every product we build, and these are the principles I hold myself to.

  1. 01

    Stay humble.

    Things can change overnight. I keep learning, and I stay open to being wrong.

  2. 02

    Keep doing it.

    No matter what, I keep going. There is always a way, and you find it by moving.

  3. 03

    Invest in the right tools.

    I put real time and money into good tools and technology. Cutting corners there always costs more later.

  4. 04

    Work with people who share the vision.

    If the vision and the values don’t match, I would rather not work together. Life is too short.

  5. 05

    Start where you are.

    Begin with what you have, toward what you want. Waiting for perfect conditions is just another way of not starting.

Nothing formal. A rough idea, a few notes or an existing product is enough. If you have competitor examples, sketches or a list of what your users struggle with, bring them along. Turning an unclear idea into a scoped plan is part of the work.
We work on fixed-milestone sprint agreements tied to agreed deliverables. Pricing depends on the functional scope, integrations and market scenario of the project. Because everything is specified upfront in the PRD, you do not face surprise hourly billing or runaway invoices. For a rough first view, the free App Cost Calculator on this site is a useful starting point.
You own 100% of the intellectual property, source code, database schemas, deployment pipelines and design assets from day one. All code is committed directly to your company's own GitHub repositories and cloud infrastructure.
