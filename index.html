FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Still need help? Let's book a call →

Do you help define the product, or only build it?
Both — and the product comes first. With eight years in product management, I help you shape scope, priorities, and a clear PRD before any code is written. Once the product is right, my team at Creuto engineers it. You can also engage us purely for product consulting if that's all you need.
How do you keep a project within budget?
By scoping honestly up front. A good PRD prevents the biggest cost driver in software — building the wrong thing. We plan a build that fits what you can invest, prioritize what matters first, and are transparent about trade-offs. No inflated quotes, no surprise costs.
What kinds of businesses and industries do you work with?
We work with B2B companies and founders across many domains — fintech, SaaS, healthcare, e-commerce, logistics, edtech and more. My product background across industries means we understand your business context, not just your feature list.
Do you have your own in-house development team?
Yes. Creuto has a dedicated in-house engineering team that builds B2B, growth-standard software directly. Your project isn't handed off to unknown subcontractors — it's built by the team you're speaking with.
How do we get started?
Start with a short consultation. Share your idea using the form above or book a call, and I'll come back with honest first thoughts on scope, approach, and budget. From there we move into a PRD and a plan. I respond within 24 hours.
