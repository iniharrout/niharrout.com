Clear expectations regarding our product process, intellectual property, timelines, and post-launch support.

We start with a 45-minute discovery consultation. We review your current operations or product concept, identify key bottlenecks, and determine whether custom software engineering makes strategic sense. If aligned, we begin with an intensive PRD and architecture specification sprint before writing production code.
Never. 100% of all software engineering, database architecture, UI/UX design, and quality assurance is conducted by our dedicated in-house team at Creuto, led directly by Nihar. You work with a unified squad with direct founder accountability.
You own 100% of all intellectual property, source code, database schemas, deployment pipelines, and design assets from day one. All code is committed directly to your company's own GitHub repositories and cloud infrastructure.
