Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers about our process, code ownership, and remote delivery.

Who will actually write the code for my product?

Creuto's full-time in-house engineering team. We never use random freelancers or outsource to unknown third-party contractors. Nihar Ranjan Rout directly oversees product specifications, sprint cadence, and engineering QA.

How do you handle time-zone differences for US or European startups?

We schedule 3 to 4 hours of daily live overlap with your time zone for sprint demos, PRD reviews, and technical alignment. The rest of the sprint operates with rigorous asynchronous discipline, daily Loom updates, and organized GitHub PRs.

Do I own the intellectual property and source code?

Yes, 100%. Upon completion and payment of milestones, all source code, database architectures, designs, and documentation are transferred directly to your organization’s GitHub repository. There are zero licensing fees or lock-ins.

What technology stack do you specialize in?

We favor modern, battle-tested, growth-standard stacks: Next.js, React, TypeScript, Node.js, Python (FastAPI/Django), PostgreSQL, Redis, Docker, and Flutter/React Native for mobile. For AI products, we implement custom LLM pipelines with LangChain, LlamaIndex, and OpenAI/Anthropic APIs.

How do we prevent scope creep and budget blowouts?

By investing time in a rigorous PRD (Product Requirement Document) before writing code. We define precise user flows, acceptance criteria, and edge cases up front. Features are prioritized so your budget goes towards what actually drives traction.

What happens after the software launches?

Every build includes a complimentary 30-day warranty period for bug fixes and performance tuning. Following launch, you can retain our team for ongoing feature sprints or transition the clean, fully-documented codebase to your internal hires.
